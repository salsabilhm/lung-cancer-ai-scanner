Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services and appointments.

How can I schedule an appointment?

You can schedule an appointment by calling our office, using our online booking system, or sending us a message through the contact form on this page.

Why is my scan not working due to image size?

If your scan is not being processed, it may be due to the image size exceeding the allowed limit. Please try resizing or compressing the image before uploading it again.

How can I make a payment in person?

You can make a payment in person by visiting our office during working hours. We accept cash, credit cards, and other available payment methods. Please check with our staff for more details.

What services do you offer?

We offer a wide range of medical services, including medical consultations and medical imaging. Please visit our website or contact us to learn more about our available services.
